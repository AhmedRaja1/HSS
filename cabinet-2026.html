Cabinet for the
Year 2026

Solemn proclamation by the Board of Governors & Alumni Council of Hazara Students Society (HSS), exercising constitutional prerogative for leadership continuity and student advocacy across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Pakistan.

📜 Constitutional Mandate & Regulations of Proclamation

  • 1
    Prerogative Under Para 4, Phase 1: Exercising Para 4, Phase 1 of the Constitution of Hazara Students Society, and seeing the past three years’ institutional performance, the Board of Governors took the decisive responsibility to officially announce the cabinet and take decisions on their own authority. In this historic matter, our esteemed alumnus Raja Ahmed took this organizational responsibility and he will be responsible for every repercussion this proclamation could entail.
  • 2
    Equal Weightage of Executive Leadership: In strict compliance with the Constitution of Hazara Students Society, the Chairpersonship and the Presidentship share equal weightage, mutual sovereignty, and co-equal executive status within the cabinet.
  • 3
    Grievance Recourse & Intra-Organization Elections: If for any reason, nevertheless, the Chairperson or President of the cabinet exhibits grievances, the Alumni and the Board of Governors shall assume direct responsibility to organize and conduct a transparent, intra-organization election to be commenced within three (3) weeks after the official announcement of the cabinet.
  • 4
    Para 6 Protocol Regarding Resignations & Redressal: As mandated under Para 6 of the Constitution of Hazara Students Society, the Alumni, the Board of Governors, and the Ex-Cabinet are not authorized to accept any resignations or process grievances until a formal delegation of the Board of Governors and Alumni arrives in Peshawar within two (2) weeks of this announcement. All grievances will be formally reviewed and conclusively redressed during an in-person delegation meeting.
  • 5
    Academic Year Fellows Term Classification: All designated cabinet officers carry verified academic year seniority notations:
    ³ Sophomore Year Fellow (3rd Term / Year)
    ⁵ Junior Year Fellow (5th Term / Year)
    ⁷ Senior Year Fellow (7th Term / Year)
