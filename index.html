Constitutional Governance

Regulated Constitution & By-Laws

A regulated, democratic constitutional charter establishing co-equal executive authority, rigorous grievance redressal, and institutional longevity.

Notice: In-Process Constitution for 2026 Uploading Soon

The updated, comprehensive Constitution of Hazara Students Society for the Year 2026 is currently undergoing final review, drafting, and formal ratification by the Board of Governors and Constitutional Steering Committee. The complete regulated text, by-laws, and chapter charters will be uploaded here shortly for public access.

Article 1 • Executive Parity

Co-Equal Weightage of Leadership

Under the Constitution of Hazara Students Society, the Chairpersonship and the Presidentship share exact equal weightage, mutual sovereign mandate, and balanced executive authority over all societal decisions and cabinet portfolios.

Article 2 • BoG Mandate (Para 4)

Board of Governors Proclamation

Exercising Para 4, Phase 1 of the Constitution, the Board of Governors reserves the authority—based on institutional performance—to directly announce the executive cabinet and steer strategic decisions, as exercised for the 2026 Cabinet by alumnus Raja Ahmed.

Article 3 • Electoral Safeguard

Intra-Organization Elections

Should the Chairperson or President exhibit formal grievance, the Alumni and Board of Governors shall hold a transparent, intra-organization election commenced within three (3) weeks of proclamation to ensure democratic legitimacy.

Article 4 • Redressal Protocol (Para 6)

In-Person Grievance Redressal

As per Para 6 of the Constitution, no resignations or grievances are entertained until an official delegation of the Board of Governors and Alumni arrives in Peshawar within two (2) weeks of announcement for formal, in-person resolution.

Article 5 • Academic Seniority

Fellows Classification Protocol

Strict seniority classification governs appointments: ³ indicates Sophomore Year Fellows (3rd term/year), ⁵ indicates Junior Year Fellows (5th term/year), and ⁷ designates Senior Year Fellows (7th term/year) safeguarding institutional mentorship.

Article 6 • Parent Chapter

UET Peshawar Sovereign Cradle

UET Peshawar remains the perpetual parent chapter and legal cradle of HSS, holding custodian authority over institutional seals, regional subsidiary charters, and provincial affiliations nationwide.

Legal Status & Compliance

Non-Profit Status & Registration in Pakistan

Hazara Students Society is actively in the process of statutory registration as per the legal statutes and regulatory framework of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

● Statutory Registration in Process

Commitment to Absolute Legal Transparency

To ensure long-term sustainability, public trust, and audited philanthropic welfare, HSS is pursuing statutory non-profit registration under the prescribed laws of Pakistan. Below are the statutory legal frameworks applicable for our registration:

Act XXI of 1860

Societies Registration Act, 1860

The foundational statutory framework in Pakistan for literary, cultural, scientific, and charitable student organizations. Provides legal personality, registered rules of governance, and formal recognition before provincial administrations.

Ordinance XLVI of 1961

Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies Ordinance, 1961

Administered by Provincial Directorates of Social Welfare for non-governmental bodies delivering student welfare funds, medical aid, disaster relief drives, and youth community development programs.

SECP • Act of 2017

Section 42, Companies Act, 2017

Incorporation as a prestigious Not-for-Profit Public Company Limited by Guarantee under license from the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), granting nationwide corporate governance and highest audit compliance.

KP Act 2020 / Act 1882

The Trusts Act, 1882 & KP Trusts Act, 2020

Establishment of an irrevocable public educational and cultural welfare trust managed by a Board of Trustees for managing scholarships, institutional bequests, and community assets.

KP Charities Act, 2019

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Charities Act, 2019

Accreditation with the KP Charity Commission governing collection, administration, and transparent accounting of student aid funds, emergency relief drives, and social welfare contributions.

Regional Struggle & Identity

The Hazara Cause — Hazara Suba

A constitutional, democratic, and administrative aspiration for equitable rights, resources, and preservation of identity.

The Constitutional Mandate for Hazara Province

The demand for a separate Hazara Province (Hazara Suba) is rooted in administrative efficiency, regional meritocracy, fair resource allocation, and the preservation of our distinct cultural, linguistic, and historical identity.

Covering eight vital districts—Haripur, Abbottabad, Mansehra, Battagram, Torghar, Upper Kohistan, Lower Kohistan, and Kolai-Palas—the Hazara region generates immense hydel power, mineral wealth, tourism revenue, and strategic geographic connectivity through the Karakoram corridor. A dedicated province ensures local administrative decentralization and youth empowerment.

Commemorated Annually • January 8

January 8 — Day of Hazara Resolution

Remembering the historic gathering, public consensus, and parliamentary resolutions championing the constitutional creation of Hazara Province as an administrative unit under Article 239 of the Constitution of Pakistan.

Solemn Tribute • April 11, 2010

April 11, 2010 — Martyrs of Abbottabad

Solemnly honoring the supreme sacrifice of the peaceful martyrs and wounded citizens of Abbottabad on April 11, 2010. Their courage and devotion to the dignity, honor, and recognition of Hazara remain etched in our collective memory forever.

Ideology & Mission

The HSS Crux & Manifesto

A cultural manifesto uniting the students of Hazara, championing our shared identity, and shaping future leadership.

Evangelizing the Vibrancy of Hazara

The Hazara region is a breathtaking tapestry of historical courage, timeless hospitality, scenic valleys, and resilient people. From Haripur to Abbottabad, Mansehra, Battagram, Torghar, and Kohistan, our culture bridges civilizations.

Hazara Students Society was established to celebrate this richness on provincial and national stages. We resist cultural homogenization by asserting the distinct voice, traditions, and pride of Hazarewal students in every academy and institution.

"Do join us if you have the vigor for your culture." — Official HSS Invitation
  • ✦

    Cultural Sovereignty

    Preserving folklore, music, traditional cuisine, attires, and communal festivities.

  • ✦

    Inter-District Unity

    Binding together students across all eight districts of Hazara Division in brotherhood.

  • ✦

    Grand Celebrations

Preserving the Sweetness of Hindko

Language is the living vessel of a people’s soul. Hindko, one of the oldest Indo-Aryan languages spoken across Hazara and Peshawar, holds immense literary depth, folk ballads, charbaitas, and oral traditions.

HSS actively champions linguistic pride, encouraging the youth to speak, write, and compose literature in Hindko and regional dialects without hesitation or inferiority.

"Language is the homeland we carry within us wherever we journey."
  • ✦

    Literary Revival

    Encouraging poetry recitations, mushairas, and publication of student folk writing.

  • ✦

    Folklore Digital Archives

    Documenting oral idioms, sayings, and classical proverbs before they fade from memory.

  • ✦

    Linguistic Inclusivity

    Fostering mutual appreciation with Pashto, Kohistani, and regional sister languages of KPK.

The UET Peshawar Legacy (Est. 1980s)

University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Peshawar holds the undisputed honor of nurturing the Hazara Students Society since the 1980s when UET became an independent chartered engineering university. For over four decades, engineers, scholars, and campus leaders at UET have spearheaded the movement.

HSS at UET Peshawar proudly acts as the parent organization for all subsidiary chapters nationwide, ensuring organizational continuity, policy standards, and institutional stability.

"UET Peshawar carries the torch of leadership that illuminates chapters across Pakistan."
